1. **Freeze a phone material tangent chart.** For each actual filled phone triangle, take a nonzero edge, project it onto the plane orthogonal to the unit body-frame outward normal `nO_body`, normalize to `t1_body`, and set `t2_body=nO_body×t1_body`. Reject degenerate triangles; do not silently switch to an adaptive world axis. Rotate the fixed basis with the phone. Alignment equations are `t1_world·nH=0` and `t2_world·nH=0`. Together with unit normals they allow either parallel branch, so `nH·nO≤−cos(1°)` selects opposition. In A0, a dot-product hinge has zero first derivative at perfectly parallel normals; keep wrong-hemisphere/stalled results explicit rather than calling normal projection alone converged.
