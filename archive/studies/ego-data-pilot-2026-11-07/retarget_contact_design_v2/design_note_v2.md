# Frame42 contact-first design

This is a source/saved-evidence design proposal, not a new optimized candidate or holding qualification. Existing v1 failures remain unchanged. The wrist transform, hand scale and anatomical digit correspondence stay fixed; permitted variables are the original 20 joints and a proper phone SE(3), without reflection.

The active human candidates are thumb and index distal skin, not their tip landmarks. Their nearest original-scan gaps are 0.031755 and 0.425184 mm, while the tip landmarks are 7.607 and 9.175 mm from the scan. Middle/ring/pinky distal gaps are 28.839/48.823/55.718 mm. These are geometric observations, not measured contacts or forces. Do not pull all five tip sites onto the phone to satisfy an equal-weight objective.

Hand2's thumb/index sensor colliders are meshes approximately 19.773×19.243×25.562 and 15.791×16.281×20.709 mm in their local frames. They are identity children of the respective distal links, separate from distal casing meshes. A tip site is only a point; neither the site nor the entire sensor chassis identifies the true compliant pad surface. Freeze a named subset of sensor surface triangles/normal range before optimization; if that patch is not established, call it a candidate collider patch. Report its actual world witness rather than assuming the human skin vertex is a robot tip target.

The minimal optimization is lexicographic:

1. Enforce source joint bounds, fixed wrist/scale, valid geometry queries, and hard phone–nonpad/table nonpenetration. Include every declared physical proxy, including source-disabled proximal proxies if whole-hand clearance is claimed; exclusions or unknown queries remain explicit. For eligible self pairs, use compiled weld/body/exclude/bitmask semantics, not a blanket XML-parent rule.
2. Enroll only the two named thumb/index patches. Proposed new design window: signed gap **0 to 0.2 mm** on each patch. This is a prospective closure tolerance, not permission to accept the old penetration. Retain the human contact neighborhood as a soft tangential witness target (about 5 mm scale); record any original-scan-to-hull projection and ties. Pad normals should oppose the phone contact normals. Positive gaps represent potential contacts after controlled closure, never forces across air.
3. Once feasible, reduce thumb/index contact error and posture cost. The other three fingers remain in the report and hard collision constraints, but their human tip-fit residuals cannot trade against contact feasibility.

Seed1 demonstrates why hard constraints matter: `right_finger2_link3_col` (index middle phalanx) penetrates 0.351132 mm, index sensor 0.004060 mm, while the thumb sensor is separated by 0.266878 mm. The all-five-tip block contributed 4.384 of the 4.977 objective. This candidate is invalid; the small tip residual of a noncontact finger does not repair it.

Use the frozen independent capsule/convex translation-gap query for canonical capsules and a declared convex/mesh query for named pad/casing geometry. Its signed penetration is fixed-orientation translation MTD of the represented convex proxies, not scan SDF or deformable tissue compression. A replacement proxy needs its own geometry/error declaration. Native seed2 thumb returned scalar zero with a **nonzero 21.054 mm witness segment**; a 14.9 nm shift changed the scalar to 20.275 mm. Such inconsistent outputs are unknown, not contact evidence. This was a supported pair, and increasing native iterations did not resolve it.

For self collision, matching collision-driver source filters equal weld IDs. Parent filtering with disableflags=0 additionally requires both weld IDs to be nonzero: fixed palm weld0 versus the first hinged link is not automatically excluded. Weld-parent IDs are obtained through the weld root's parent. Automatic pairs also apply masks and body exclude signatures. Explicit pairs enter separately and can bypass those automatic masks/exclusions, so retain their route and eligibility explicitly. Natural same-weld pad/casing overlap is not a self-collision violation.

The pure `static_support_lp.py` now checks conditional point-contact statics. Nominal **mass 0.2 kg and sliding friction 0.7 are assumptions**. Its default four rays are `n ± μt1`, `n ± μt2`, with force normal on the object. A geometry-query normal pointing phone→hand must be negated. Match actual native tangent axes before calling the cone engine-equivalent; a deterministic basis is only a specified cone. The optional eight-ray circular approximation is sensitivity analysis. No torsional/rolling moment or unregistered finger/table support is introduced.

The equations are Σf+mg=0, Σ(r−COM)×f=0 and τ_act=bias+ΣJᵀf_object. The latter sign includes the hand's opposite contact reaction. Actual zero-speed bias/J and reachable torque intervals must come from later authorized source/dynamics evidence. Derive bounds from actual PD command ranges, gear and actuator/joint clamps, not only symmetric force caps; do not use rounded XML gains. Static frictionloss assistance is omitted. Two point contacts cannot supply every moment along their connecting line: infeasible point statics is not a proof that a finite contact patch cannot hold.

For a future condim3/pyramidal experiment, explicitly declare each enrolled pair's five friction coefficients, solref/solimp, margin/gap and dimension; the hand's priority1 material otherwise overrides the priority0 phone settings. A prospective matched setting is friction `[0.7,0.7,0,0,0]`, solref `[0.0025,1]`, solimp `[0.9,0.95,0.001,0.5,2]`, margin=gap=0. These remain assumptions for Root's new protocol. Condim4 torsion or multiple patch points require a separate support model.

Four analytic fixtures (five LP calls) passed: opposed frictional support with analytic minimum squeeze; eccentric one-point torque failure; actuator-limited failure; and one point below COM with up/down normal success/failure. No Hand2 J, bias, FK, optimization, simulation or new candidate force was evaluated. Next evidence must be controlled closure followed by an unforced object gravity/perturbation test under actual joint limits. LP feasibility alone never certifies a stable grasp, calibrated friction or ego retargeting success.
