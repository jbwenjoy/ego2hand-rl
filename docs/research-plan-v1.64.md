# Research plan v1.64 — prospective switch sampling and reference-backend identity

Four frozen policies each supplied 144 candidate controls from phase 96 through 239 on the known pen reference. The source-only score uses signed separations and instantaneous normal velocities of eligible finger-2/3 and pen geom pairs. Each 12-control stratum contributed its highest score and one uniform phase drawn before execution. Shared selected phases were replayed only once. Four full-state restores per chosen source supplied two sham and two opposite mixed actions. Previous fit/test actor ownership is preserved. All 93 unique sources, 372 branches and 10,624 native integrations are accounted for. Source motion and scene limits are properties of these policies and the one reference.

There were three overlapping selections, six strata positive with either selection, eight positive only at the risk-selected point and four positive only at the uniform point for the whole-hand interventional criterion. On the full 576-source census, 180 nominal trajectories changed pair membership within the eight pre-step geometries. The nominal-event classifier produced the same heldout binary alarms as signed-gap extrapolation at its training-selected threshold. Geometry-pair+event-type precision/recall were 11/13 and 11/22 (residual) and 14/18 and 14/47 (support). It improved the ranking of event-prone windows, especially on the residual actor. This nominal event task differs from predicting which action causes a switch. A matched-size nearest-source capacity-contrast estimator did not show consistent heldout advantage from the enriched data. A second finding affects the interpretation of geometry oracles. Replaying the measured substep poses with the guarded GPU forward graph reproduced all 720 contact-geom multisets on the two heldout cohorts and their pooled capacity response exactly at reported precision. Using predicted secant poses instead gave relative errors 1.36 and 2.51. Full-scene CPU contact generation at measured poses gave 0.982 and 0.992. In an oracle using observed membership to replace missing/extra CPU geom groups, errors fell to 0.137 and 0.062. Switching the diagnostic contact shape from the pyramid to an elliptic cone did not resolve the discrepancy. Across the larger 2,976-tick audit CPU midphase removal restored all 689 Warp-only memberships, but added extra CPU-only groups and left multiplicity differences. On the heldout cohorts disabling CPU midphase alone gave pooled errors 1.06 and 0.70. This result is specific to the pinned compiled scene, data and query paths. Of 3,947 defined nominal wrench intervals, 28 passed both GPU geometry and the instantaneous force-cone screen (CPU 22, one unresolved LP previously). There are 733 geometry-eligible capacity-supervision intervals across the catalog versus 1,374 from the CPU scene. Different per-frame gates may offset in aggregate counts. On 001 eligibility changed from 185 to 176; neither backend produced an interval passing both the geometry and nominal wrench feasibility gates. On the actual frozen A1/A2 endpoints, rescoring the full eight-tick means against GPU references gave A2 minus A1 mismatch +0.000675 and +0.000244 on the two complete common intervals (seeds 7412,7413). Seed 7411 had no eligible window before its common cutoff. This rescoring changes no original training, raw data or strict task outcomes.
