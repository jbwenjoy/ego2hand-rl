# Research plan v1.59 — contact feature coverage and persistence increments

The paired ablation added the previous load to the network output for the first 12 channels and retained the previous 52-channel target scaling, model size, optimizer, training budget and validation rule. The selected original-data model reached 2.21° and 1.99° pose RMS at 80 ms for the two residual-policy cohorts. On the support-policy cohort it reached 82.62°, with force RMS 0.605 N against 0.0284 N for persistence. See [the fixed-split fit](../runs/pen_load_increment_v1/report.json).

All 3,744 training examples had exactly zero prior normal load on finger 5 and the other-hand category. In 159 of the 367 support test examples a training-invariant channel changed, with finger 5 reaching 0.595 N. We held every trained weight fixed, masking only the constant columns in the nonlinear correction while keeping the measured history in the additive path. The test protects that distinction. Force RMS dropped to 0.0375 N and the 80 ms pose RMS to 12.19°. At 320 ms the result still drifted to 712 mm and 101.56°. The force action-response JVP error ratio on ten support-source pairs was 1.055 relative to zero predicted response. This intervention identifies a source of extrapolation in the learned predictor; its scope is previously inspected single-reference cohorts. See [feature ablation](../runs/pen_supported_increment_v1/report.json) and [paired derivatives](../runs/pen_supported_increment_v1/native_gradient/report.json).

Twelve fresh trajectories mixed only existing training actors, with an eight-control transfer to a separate earlier support policy. The 509 valid post-switch examples add fifth-finger load up to 0.197 N. They reduced unsupported prior contact-mask rows on the support cohort from 137 to 16. With matched optimizer calls and training samples per epoch, the augmented increment model selected epoch 40. Its support force RMS was 0.133 N and its 80 ms orientation RMS 17.32°. Coverage of the binary load mask alone was insufficient. The online collector did not alter the actor weights. See [collection](../runs/pen_switch_training_v1/report.json) and [matched fit](../runs/pen_switch_increment_v1/report.json).

For further validation, separate the responding contact geometry and moment arm from coarse finger masks; compare action-conditioned force increments and derivatives with physics on a fresh behavior and reference.
