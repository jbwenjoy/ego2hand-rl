# Pen shaft suggestions from a learned contact model — local vs full-reference effects

A fixed state-only load network predicts source-BODY force, torque and digit loads from state55 and an actual joint target. A differentiable rigid integration advances object linear and angular momentum and pose; the hand increment is learned. The original outer RAW clamp, residual scaling, EMA, encoder bias and joint target bounds are included. The three-control diagnostic differentiates average directed shaft loss (one minus the axis dot product). Only the first RAW is perturbed and only in symmetric unsaturated directions of fingers 2 and 3. The next two RAW vectors remain the same clipped baseline in every branch. This isolates an action suggestion. The actor, its normalizer and the model stay fixed.

On ten native snapshots per policy, two repetitions each of baseline, descent, and equal-norm ascent produced 360 scored native control intervals. Every branch was restored to its own source. All sources and windows passed the original physical checks. Independent reconstruction found that descent beat baseline and ascent beyond baseline repeat spread at ten of ten residual sources and seven of ten support sources. The median three-control mean shaft angle improvement was 0.086° and 0.024°, respectively. On residual sources the median native total reward change was negative (−0.00053). The chosen shaft objective and the full task reward are distinct. See [independent review](../runs/pen_load_suggestions_review_v1/report.json).

A separate W2048 continuation Actor was frozen and evaluated continuously from reset. Over controls 206–222 only, its original, descent and ascent actions were compared with the same 0.02 RAW norm rule (three numerical repeats per arm). At 4.44 s, shaft errors were 51.08–51.21°, 50.24–50.27°, and 52.40–52.47°. All nine completed the reference with strict full-task success zero. Full-reference SO3 RMS ranges overlap (40.11–40.13°, 40.08–40.13°, 40.09–40.16°). There is evidence for a small local corrective effect and no established full-task gain. This is one controlled reference and a previously inspected family of policies. See [continuous interventions](../runs/pen_load_suggestion_policy_v1/report.json).

The differentiable 161-feature observation reconstructs the separate saved native Actor input within 2.14e-7 on 12 observations and preserves the previous applied RAW action. Together with the tested differentiable step, it supports future reference-conditioned Actor updates. An actual paired learned-policy optimization and independent seed/reference evaluation are separate outstanding experiments.
