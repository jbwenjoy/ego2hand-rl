# Research plan v1.58 — load prediction and policy gradients

The guarded W32 restore and independent contact checks are complete. Both 256-world Actor arms trained for 2,781 iterations with equal transition and optimizer budgets. Both final policies completed all three 7.36 s reference executions and failed the strict orientation tolerance. Larger-batch PPO continuation reached 56,585 valid transitions/s at 4096 worlds and 32 Adam updates per rollout. These results use one controlled reference. See the [batch experiment](../runs/pen_batch_updates_v1/comparison.json) and [paired support run](../runs/pen_scaled_support_matched_v1/report.json).

Sixteen fresh W1 episodes yielded 5,228 valid controls and 41,824 native integrations. The load forecaster uses all twenty measured joint targets and a 55-dimensional state. The history variant also consumes the preceding control's mean force, torque and digit normal loads, expressed in the current source frame. Training used 3,744 adjacent valid examples from four policies, validation used 367 from a fifth, and three other policies each supplied 367 testing examples. The policies share a reference, and some share training ancestry. Previous work inspected these behaviors. See the [sequence manifest](../runs/guarded_load_sequences_v1/report.json).

At 80 ms the history model reduced orientation RMS to 2.42° and 2.17° on two residual policies (matched constant velocity 4.85° and 4.65°). On the final support policy it increased RMS to 72.50° compared with 12.23° for state and PD alone. Carrying the preceding force on that policy gave 0.0284 N one-step force RMS compared with 0.1321 N and 0.5242 N for the learned variants. The policy with support reward encountered 32 digit load-mask transitions in the scored 367 samples, versus nine in each residual cohort. These observations do not isolate the cause. At 320 ms only the state-only variant stayed below 12° on both residual cohorts. Test windows overlap. See the [forecasts](../runs/pen_load_forecast_v1/report.json) and [stratified comparisons](../runs/pen_load_forecast_strata_v1/report.json).

Independent native paired perturbations exercise one mixed direction across eight loaded-finger PD coordinates at ten states per behavior. For the history model the support force and normal-load JVP error ratios against a zero-change baseline were 1.09 and 1.41. Lower one-step forecast error on a residual policy therefore does not qualify this model for broad policy-gradient use. See the [native action response checks](../runs/pen_load_forecast_gradients_v1/report.json).

Next collect additional training-only grasp transitions in the load range reached by the support policy, measure feature extrapolation before assigning cause, and retain a separately owned evaluation policy and additional references. Compare persistence-residual contact heads against the current forecasters using single-step forces, native directional derivatives, and closed state propagation with recorded controls. True A1/A2, multireference and real ego dynamics validation, and hardware observation/control verification remain open.
