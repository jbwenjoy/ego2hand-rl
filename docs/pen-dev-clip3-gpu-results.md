# Multiclip GPU development result, 2026-11-20

Three fresh numerical trials for each clip/servo-target combination used the guarded W1 solver, the saved mount and joint model, zero initial velocity, and 80 fixed-control ticks. Contact reconstruction was checked every eight ticks. In clip 003 the preload reduced maximum COM drift from 62.52 mm to 3.77 mm and peak orientation drift from approximately 180° to 9.22°. Every preloaded window passed the physical checks and every tick had more than 0.1 N hand contact. In clip 004 preload left 52.68–52.69 mm translation and roughly 179° rotation; only eight of ten windows passed. Full-hold drift includes subsequent motion even when an audit failed. The clip 003 development adapter preserves the original observation, action, reward, reset and native step implementations while binding the frame count, fixed-wrist reference and preload to this clip. Source qpos and all reference joints are checked at construction, as are the reference COM and orientation over the complete 373-frame trajectory. Preload initializes the servo target and EMA; subsequent actions come from the unmodified 256-world trained Actor. Each fresh W1 starts at phase 1. Physical failures stop on the first invalid control under the unchanged 2 mm object penetration threshold.

The residual policy violated that threshold at its first control (2.0292 mm) in each of three trials. The support policy passed 50 controls (1.00 s), then violated the same gate at control 51 (2.663–2.959 mm). The terminal windows passed the independent EFC numerical checks and the joint/self geometry gates. Neither arm reached strict full-reference success on this 7.44 s development clip. This small transfer comparison concerns one development clip and common policy seed. Validation and reserve clips have not been policy-scored.
