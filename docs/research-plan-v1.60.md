# Research plan v1.60 — controlled reference catalog and candidate resets

The local single-axis catalog has ten indexed, hash-verified clips at 50 Hz. In fixed-wrist coordinates, use each recorded wrist pose to remove the source wrist motion and compose the resulting relative object pose with the robot mount. On clip 001 the recomposed world position and orientation match the archived evaluation reference within 1 micrometer and 1e-5 rad respectively. Clip 001 remains the original training example. Slots 002–006 serve development, 007–008 validation, and 009–010 are reserved for later policy evaluation. Every clip received geometry screening, and no policy outcome selected the split. Different clip files alone do not certify independent demonstrations or held-out subject generalization.

[Catalog screen](../runs/pen_catalog_screen_v2/report.json): 3,987 frames; 1,603 met the original penetration and joint gates. Of 3,947 nominal interval load targets, 43 had a verified instantaneous friction-pyramid solution at source-frame penetrating/touching robot-object contact points, and 22 also passed geometry. One LP was numerically unresolved and is retained separately. The first attempt halted on that solver status; the completed screen flags the interval. The cone test uses unlimited normal forces, an estimated mean wrench, and a single geometry sample. Positive and negative results describe that approximation. The maximum difference between source link references and model body origins is 4.1–4.9 mm across clips, which requires further geometry/landmark correspondence work.

[Development projection](../runs/pen_dev_projection_v1/report.json): all five source first frames exceeded the original 2 mm object penetration limit. Joint-only projections respecting the predeclared fingers 1/2/3 and a 0.10 rad per-joint bound passed the stricter projection geometry gates on all five. In stationary 200 ms CPU servo holds, projected poses drifted 47–82 mm. These positions require dynamic initialization work.

[Static preload experiment](../runs/pen_dev_static_preload_v1/report.json): calculate equal/opposite contact wrenches consistent with gravity and map the reaction on each hand body to joint torques at the same contact point. Only two of five projected poses had a static cone solution. On clip 003 a capped servo target offset reduced maximum drift from 67.46 mm and 178.47° to 6.81 mm and 11.62°. On clip 004 the angular error remained about 179°. These CPU holds have fixed targets and no externally applied object force. Complete reference tracking, guarded GPU equivalence and cross-clip policy performance remain open.

Next verify candidate preload, collision parameters and joint targets against the guarded W1 GPU runtime for development clips, starting with the informative 003/004 contrast. Preserve the frozen 001 initializer and separate physical qualification from the later 007–010 policy tests. Keep v1.59 load-model, true A1/A2, multi-seed, real ego and hardware objectives active.
