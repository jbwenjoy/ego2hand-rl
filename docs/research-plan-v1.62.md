# Research plan v1.62 — native capacity response supervision

The frozen geometry proxy from v1.61 is evaluated under small changes of the eight D2/D3 joint actions. The [fresh native bank](../runs/pen_capacity_pair_labels_v1/report.json) has 600 eight-tick packets from 30 exact source snapshots and 10,200 native integrations including the prefixes. Two policies own training data, one policy supplies a holdout. All mixed actions are excluded from fitting. Each of two heads receives the same 3,675 sequence rows and 360 added absolute endpoints. The response variant also learns 159 same-source basis contrasts; one clipped pair had zero effective PD contrast. Both heads have identical initial weights, minibatch order and 1,740 Adam updates. Comparisons with earlier results on these previously inspected policies are exploratory.

On the TEST policy's ten held-out mixed directions the pooled capacity-vector JVP error ratio to a zero-change prediction fell from 1.284 to 1.125. Directional dot products were positive in 4 and 5 cases respectively. Absolute endpoint MAE increased from 0.01039 to 0.01226. The eligible scalar target-mismatch response ratio fell from 1.536 to 0.987; signs were correct in 5/8 in each arm. External checks using directions chosen by a shaft-angle objective found small amplitude ratios for the response-supervised head (0.060 residual, 0.022 support); this is a separate directional test. [Fit](../runs/pen_capacity_pair_fit_v1/report.json) · [external check](../runs/pen_capacity_external_model_review_v1/report.json).

For prospective validation both heads issued equal-amplitude suggestions before physical branches, starting from fresh W1 states. The new residual Actor had not supplied fitting data, and the support cohort remains an inspected development policy. Each arm had eight eligible phases out of ten. Across 4,880 native ticks including prefixes, all eligible groups completed. The absolute and response heads both won 2/8 on the new residual behavior and 3/8 on the support behavior, using the stricter criterion of beating baseline and opposite beyond the two baseline repeats' spread. Median response-head improvement was −0.000031 and +0.000242 respectively. Thus the fitted relative gains on training behaviors do not justify a longer A2 Actor run. [Native mechanism comparison](../runs/pen_paired_capacity_suggestions_v1/report.json).

On the TEST basis probes six of 80 pairs changed at least one nonpositive-gap contact geom-pair multiplicity and carried 92.4% of squared 72-vector change. Removing mismatching geom groups symmetrically reduces the pooled RMS to 27.9% of its original value. This is a descriptor sensitivity test with no change to the physical trajectories. It does not infer discontinuity of the physical wrench or absence of facet changes for unchanged pair IDs. [Topology check](../runs/pen_capacity_topology_contrast_v1/report.json).

Using measured GPU substep poses with the same CPU geometry query yields 0.179 relative mixed-response error; interpolating measured start/end object and hand gives 0.185. Replacing the hand endpoint alone gives 0.913, the object endpoint alone 1.351, and both 1.362. Open-loop CPU dynamics driven only by the initial native integration state and held PD yields 0.876 on these mixed pairs and 0.122 on the 80 basis pairs. The former measured-state conditions are privileged diagnostics. A post-hoc CPU selector simulating the five predeclared available actions from their shared initial state selected native wins in 6/8 groups on each behavior; in the remaining 2/8 it did worse than baseline. This is a more expensive selector, with no independent full-trajectory or learned-policy evidence.
