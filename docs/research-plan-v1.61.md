# Research plan v1.61 — physically checked model suggestions and paired Actor pilot

The native controlled tasks, source and physics contracts, measured scaling studies, manipulation demonstrations and remaining M0–M5 objectives in v1.60 still apply. The complete-task gates and split assignments are unchanged. New evidence is divided into three different kinds of result below.

**Model-selected actions.** On the guarded single-pen reference we measured the *direction the load model itself selects*, its opposite and two unperturbed native repeats from the same complete source snapshot. Native improvement beyond repeat spread was observed on 10/10 residual and 7/10 support-policy sources for the three-step shaft objective. In a different residual continuation policy, applying these suggestions at controls 206–222 reduced the 4.44 s shaft angle by roughly 0.9°, with overlapping full-trajectory RMS and strict success 0/3. [Method and results](pen-shaft-suggestion-results-2026-11-20.md).

**CHORD-style geometry target.** A friction-pyramid contact row gives unit-normal generators expressed in the pen BODY frame about its moving COM. A shared total normal budget of 1 N and length scale 0.10 m give comparable coordinates for force and moment. The support function is sampled in 72 antipodally balanced directions. Duplicate rows cannot create extra capacity. This measures the shape of a geometric capability set without assuming human force measurements. Native realized normalized wrenches satisfied the sampled support inequalities. A reference interval receives positive supervision only when both frames pass the prior geometry screen, at least one hand contact exists, and the set of fingers in nonpositive-gap contact is stable. Slow and turning phases retain their own target. Switching and inadmissible intervals have zero weight, which avoids prescribing persistent squeezing. On clip 001, 185/368 intervals receive supervision (145 slow, 40 turning). In the earlier exact nominal-wrench test no interval jointly satisfied all geometry and nominal load-feasibility gates on this clip. Matching the capacity descriptor therefore does not certify physical reference feasibility. [Catalog and native checks](../runs/pen_capacity_targets_v1/report.json).

**Capacity predictions.** A fresh 5,069-control, 40,552-integration bank retains per-tick geometry labels, preceding action/state alignment and policy-owned train/validation/test splits. On the three test policies the state/history heads both remained worse in mean component error than previous-window persistence. The geometry-consistent 12-generator head removed triangle-bound violations of unconstrained directional outputs but worsened component accuracy. In the state head, on shaft-selected equal/opposite actions, the magnitude of predicted capacity-mismatch response was only 15.6% and 2.3% of measured response for the residual/support cohorts. On the capacity head's own eligible suggestions, the stricter test (both repetitions beat baseline and opposite sign beyond baseline spread) yielded only 2/8 and 4/8 wins, respectively. All 16 eligible groups passed native checks. The zero-action baseline and predicted direction are gauged at the same source. Capacity targets remain an investigational reward. [Fit](../runs/pen_capacity_fit_v1/report.json) · [structured ablation](../runs/pen_generator_capacity_fit_v1/report.json) · [native directional check](../runs/pen_native_capacity_suggestions_v1/report.json).

**Imagined Actor update.** Thirty TRAIN-only start snapshots have verified 161-feature inputs, action history, bounds and three reference frames per start. The paired pilot uses one common parent and differentiable three-step physical rollout, with 24 Adam calls per arm per sampling seed and a norm constraint on the selected start states. A1 and A2 use identical mini-batches and dynamics; A2 adds eligible finite-capacity mismatch. Updates are confined to the final-layer mean rows for two loaded fingers. These are short diagnostic fine-tunes with three sampling orders, not independent full model/Actor training seeds. Results in the simulator must distinguish physical stops from task stops and report complete horizons separately from common valid prefixes. [Pilot protocol and checkpoints](../runs/pen_paired_imagination_v1/intent.json). At initialization the added capacity term is 3.28% of the pooled base gradient norm, and A1 vs A2 gradient cosine is 0.99948 before Adam and projection. The scalar loss improvement in imagination does not establish a policy gain. [Gradient balance](../runs/pen_imagination_gradient_balance_v1/report.json).
